# Credits

## Morrowlamp Game Workshop

**Created and orchestrated by Christopher Davis**

Christopher Davis conceived and directed the Morrowlamp project, established its technical
and creative constraints, selected and integrated implementations, evaluated iterations,
directed architectural changes, and managed its multi-agent development and verification
process.

Morrowlamp is intentionally a human-directed, multi-agent software and creative experiment.
Generative AI systems were used in different roles including software implementation, code
review, architecture analysis, game writing and revision, procedural art development,
documentation, testing, independent auditing, and concept exploration.

The Sunken Crown is Morrowlamp's flagship reference cartridge. Its fictional narrative and
much of its initial game content were generated and iteratively revised through AI-assisted
development under human direction. Christopher Davis does not claim to have personally
written the game's fictional narrative.

Morrowlamp is part software-engineering experiment, part creative-tool prototype, and part
art project exploring what one person can create by directing, evaluating, combining, and
independently verifying the work of multiple generative systems.
