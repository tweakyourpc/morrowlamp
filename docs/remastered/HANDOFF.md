## Rules that are easy to break

- Shells present; the Machine decides. Nothing in `js/remastered/` may change game state except
  through the runtime.
- Music has no audio files: everything is synthesized. Voice clips are found by the hash of
  their text, so an edited line needs re-voicing and the old clip is pruned.
- Allocate ports with portbroker and bind 0.0.0.0. Do not download external code without
  Chris's OK. Commit only when asked.
- legion (`ssh showrig-worker`) sleeps and drops off the network; Blender 5.3 lives at
  `~/opt/blender-5.3` there. The local Blender is 4.0.2, CPU only (about 1 hour a room).
- GPU-capable Blender/render work goes to legion first through `batch_render.py` or
  `remote_render.sh`; use this machine only after confirming legion is unreachable.

## Known rough edges

The hero is a simple model; hall floorboards read as large tiles; Whisper sometimes mishears a
good take (the WER flag is advisory); the device voice depends on the browser's voices; the
`?score=chip` mode ignores moods. The tunnel and the brass passage have nothing to handle, and
`wake: "look"` needs a targeted action, so with "Rooms: wake" they may never wake (needs Chris's
call: wake on entry, or stay retro). The vault's way out is seen edge-on, so it is mostly a click
zone on the right edge. The guardroom floor shows a dark wedge from the fill light.
