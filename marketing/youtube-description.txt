Description:
Your grandmother left you a house. Under the hill it stands on, a kingdom sank one night with its king and his crown, and nobody has found them since. Now the mist has come, just as she said it would.

The Sunken Crown is a point-and-click adventure in the spirit of the Infocom text adventures and the EGA role-playing games of the 1980s. Explore Ashcombe Manor and the drowned halls beneath it, recover the six lost treasures, and find out what really happened to the Sunken King.

Every room begins as it would have looked on a 1987 home computer. The first time you do something there, it wakes into full modern light, so the house fills in as you play.

Play free, no download or account needed: https://tweakyourpc.github.io/morrowlamp/remastered.html

What's in it:
- 21 rooms, from the manor and its overgrown kitchen garden to the halls under the hill
- Click to play, or type commands the old-fashioned way
- Fully narrated, with a choice of three narrators (or play text only)
- Rooms in classic EGA, CGA or VGA that wake into modern renders, or modern from the start
- An original score that plays as you go
- Your game saves itself, and a save code lets you carry on in any browser
- Runs in any modern browser on desktop or laptop

How it was made:
The Sunken Crown is free and open source (MIT). I made it with open-source tools: Blender for the rooms, three.js in the browser, Piper for the voices (trained on public-domain LibriVox recordings), and a score written as code. I used AI assistance (Claude Code) for programming and procedural art, and the full credits are in the game under "About & credits".

Arcade veterans: one famous cheat still works in these halls.
